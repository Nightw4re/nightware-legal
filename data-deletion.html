Foundrysteed

Data Deletion Information

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This page explains deletion options for Foundrysteed, developed and published by Nightware Games. Read it together with our Privacy Policy.

1. Accounts and Google Play Games

Foundrysteed does not have a separate Nightware Games username-and-password account registration or an in-game “Delete Account” feature. The Android game supports Google Play Games authentication and Saved Games backups associated with your Google Play Games profile. A Google account and its service data are managed by Google, not by deleting a local Foundrysteed installation.

2. Delete locally stored progress

Local app data includes settlement progress, resources, buildings, upgrades, settings and purchase-fulfillment records. To remove the app’s local storage on Android:

  1. Close Foundrysteed and open your device’s Settings.
  2. Open Apps and select Foundrysteed.
  3. Open Storage or Storage & cache.
  4. Select Clear storage or Clear data and confirm. Menu names vary by device. Clearing only the cache is not the same operation.

This removes local progress and settings. It does not delete Google Play Games backups, Google purchase records or advertising-service data. Exported save files, if any, must be deleted separately from wherever you kept them. Android backup/restoration or restoring a Play Games backup can bring progress back.

Uninstalling removes the installed app and its local app storage, but is not a request to delete information held by Google or other services. Device backups may remain. If you want to prevent data being restored, also review the backup and Play Games settings for your device/account.

3. Google Play Games and cloud data

The Game supports manual and automatic Google Play Games backups when authenticated. Clearing local app data does not send a deletion request for those backups. Foundrysteed does not currently provide an in-app control to delete Google-held cloud saves or your Google account.

Use Google’s instructions for deleting Play Games data or a Play Games profile. They include a web route through your Play Games profile: open Your Data, then the account/data deletion controls, and find Foundrysteed under individual game data. Names and available controls may vary; follow Google’s current instructions.

Deleting Play Games data may affect game progress and achievements. It does not necessarily delete Play History, purchase records, information retained by other Google services, or records separately held by a developer. For problems with data controlled by Google, use Google’s privacy and support controls; Nightware Games cannot delete your Google account for you.

4. Request deletion from Nightware Games

For questions or deletion requests concerning information controlled by Nightware Games, email support.nightware@gmail.com with the subject Foundrysteed data deletion request.

Describe the information or previous support conversation your request concerns. We can receive your email address and any information you choose to include. Please do not send passwords, full payment card details or unnecessary save files. We may need enough information to identify the records and verify that the request concerns you.

This is a route to contact the developer about data under its control, not a remote button for clearing your device or deleting Google-held information. For locally stored data, follow section 2; for Google service data, use the relevant Google controls. The applicable service’s retention and deletion rules may continue to apply.

5. Purchases, advertising and other third-party data

Google Play Billing manages purchases and payment records. Removing local data or contacting Nightware Games does not erase Google’s transaction records, cancel a purchase or guarantee a refund. Use Google Play’s purchase and support controls for those matters.

Google AdMob and platform services may process identifiers, network information, advertising interactions and diagnostics. Review Google’s Privacy Policy and your Google/Android privacy controls for requests concerning those services. Resetting or deleting an advertising identifier does not itself erase every historical service record.

For an overview of the Game’s services and data handling, return to the Privacy Policy.
